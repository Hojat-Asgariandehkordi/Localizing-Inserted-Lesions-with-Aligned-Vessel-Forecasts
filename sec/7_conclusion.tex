\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}
We asked whether a vessel forecast helps a nodule scorer by being placed correctly or by adding information. Paired crops and a rotation sham answer it: on held-out data, alignment changes the location the scorer returns far more than the decision it makes, and plain fixed subtraction suffices. The gain also holds on expert-annotated native vessels.

All positive targets are inserted signals, so real nodules, scan-level pipelines, partial-volume effects and per-scan false-positive rates remain untested. The prespecified crop-level criterion was not met, the localization robustness checks were conducted post hoc, and absolute localized sensitivity remains modest. Next steps are a prespecified scan-level evaluation on adjudicated real nodules with localized sensitivity as the primary endpoint, and learned scorers that use an aligned forecast explicitly. Placement shams are a simple control for claims that spatial context helps.
