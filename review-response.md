The fixed-minus-Hessian interval spans zero. The broader holdout likewise gives a profile-minus-fixed crop difference of −0.74 pp (95% CI −3.04 to +0.54). **New evidence for spatial correspondence.** The original locked placement gain remains +9.37 pp in crop sensitivity at the matched cap (95% CI +4.56 to +12.29). This endpoint requires the accepted crop's returned mark to be within 3 mm of the inserted target. The broad holdout contains useful moves on crops accepted by both methods. The main comparison retains the smaller crop-level effects and shows which cases gain.

A prospective advantage for choosing among interventions at equal total cost remains open. The independent native-vessel result uses inserted targets. This is a credible accept case as a controlled localization study. The missed primary crop criterion and the remaining policy and clinical gaps still make an 8+/10 strong-accept assessment difficult to justify on the evidence alone.
